PricePulse

A full-stack web platform that tracks, compares and visualises supermarket prices across Irish retailers — making everyday inflation visible.

Comparative & Historical Price Analysis Platform for Supermarket Products Using Web Technologies

GitHub Repo

About

Fighting price creep with data.

84% of Irish consumers are worried about the cost-of-living crisis. Yet most supermarket tools only show today's prices. PricePulse tracks prices over time, making the slow creep of inflation impossible to ignore.

The Problem

Consumers are finding the rise of the cost of living increasingly difficult to manage, with everyday essentials seeing steady price increases. Families with smaller budgets are hit hardest by these changes.

The Solution

PricePulse shows up-to-date prices on products from Dunnes Stores and SuperValu, and renders them as interactive historical charts so users can see exactly when prices change and when deals are happening.

Historical Data

PricePulse maintains a comprehensive database of historical prices and sales data, allowing users to analyze trends and make informed purchasing decisions.

Personalisation

Registered users can save a personal watchlist of frequently purchased items, turning PricePulse into a dashboard for keeping tabs on the price of their shopping basket.

Architecture

Decoupled, layer by layer.

Each tier of the system operates independently through well-defined interfaces. Updates to one layer, such as patching a scraper, do not require changes anywhere else.

Frontend

Single Page Application with interactive price history charts and responsive design.

SMART Objectives

Five goals. All delivered.

The project was scoped around five measurable objectives set at the outset. All five were met at the level of core functionality.

01

Interactive web application — a searchable frontend enabling cross-retailer price comparison across Dunnes Stores and SuperValu.

02

Historical price tracking — an automated daily scraping pipeline writing timestamped records to MongoDB, visualised as interactive line charts.

03

Scalable database schema — a MongoDB document model with a curated cross-retailer product registry for reliable grouping and querying.

04

User accounts and wishlist — registered users can save products to a persistent personal watchlist with full price history access.

05

GDPR and ethical compliance — data minimisation, bcrypt password hashing, JWT sessions, and full ethics approval from the SETU School of Science and Computing.
